Project Notes: Stage 3

From facade to graph

This project looks at inferencing the interior layout from the facade geometry of HDB building modules. This is done by extracting the geometry into a graph representation and then inferencing the interior graph based on the exterior graph.

STAGE 04 / MATERIAL ASSESSMENT

Embodied carbon and material cost calculation

Use the predicted interior layout to derive material quantities, then combine these with embodied carbon factors and material unit costs to estimate the impact and cost of the inferred construction.

Predicted layout → Material quantities → Embodied carbon + material cost
