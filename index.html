A Research for Computational Architecture using Building Data

Aries Yang is a PhD candidate at the National University of Singapore working across generative design, AI-assisted design modelling, graph-based spatial inference, and computational architecture.

This site treats projects as rooms: selected research can be opened, compared, and read through its evidence, images, methods, and dates.

R-01 X: FACADE Y: GRAPH
2026 CAADRIA Image-to-graph framework

Exterior to Interior: An Image-to-Graph Framework for Predicting Structural Graphs from Facade Images

A research project connecting facade imagery, photogrammetry, detection models, synthetic data, and graph neural networks to infer spatial and structural information from visible building data.

R-02 X: HDB Y: WALLS
2025 HDB research Graph neural networks

Inferring Internal Wall Configurations in HDBs Using Heterogeneous Graph Neural Networks

A research sequence using graph-based prediction, error analysis, validation overlays, and cost / embodied-carbon evaluation to study hidden internal spatial conditions.

R-03 X: ROOM Y: MODEL
2026 Segmentation 3D interior reconstruction

Interior Segmentation and AI-Assisted 3D Spatial Capture

A set of visual experiments around interior segmentation, AI-assisted object recognition, and 3D reconstruction using provided spatial image assets.
